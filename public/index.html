How it works

1. VYRO fetches the latest Solana pools from GeckoTerminal.
2. When started, the engine checks newly appearing pool IDs against your risk and position limits.
3. Matching pairs are paper-bought using virtual SOL.
4. Take-profit and stop-loss are checked after fresh market data updates.
5. Stop prevents new automated entries; it does not close open positions.
